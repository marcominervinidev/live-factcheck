## 4. TV talk shows, local only

- Shows: Lanz and Illner (ZDF); Maischberger, Hart aber fair and Caren Miosga (ARD; Anne Will ended on 2023-12-03, [horizont](https://www.horizont.net/medien/nachrichten/ard-polittalk-anne-will-moderiert-zum-letzten-mal-anne-will-216337)); Phoenix Runde (phoenix).
- Terms of [ARD](https://www.ardmediathek.de/nutzungsbedingungen) and [ZDF](https://www.zdf.de/nutzungsbedingungen): copying or storing "bedarf der vorherigen schriftlichen Zustimmung". Use is allowed only "zu privaten, nichtkommerziellen Zwecken unter Einhaltung des (geltenden) Urheberrechts".
- [§ 53(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__53.html): a natural person may make single copies for private use, with no direct or indirect commercial purpose, from a lawful source. Mediathek files are said to be unencrypted ([MediathekView forum, 2018](https://forum.mediathekview.de/post/6251)). Breaking DRM would violate [§ 95a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__95a.html).
- [§ 44b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__44b.html): copies for text and data mining (TDM) are allowed unless the rights holder reserved them in machine-readable form, and the copies must be deleted once no longer needed. ARD reserves: robots.txt "reserves the right pursuant to § 44b(3) UrhG … to prohibit the use of its content for text and data mining" (Stand 19.05.2026, [ardmediathek.de](https://www.ardmediathek.de/robots.txt), same text on ard.de and ardaudiothek.de). ZDF has no such line in its [robots.txt](https://www.zdf.de/robots.txt), its `/.well-known/tdmrep.json` returns 404 and its terms have no TDM clause. phoenix: nothing in its [robots.txt](https://www.phoenix.de/robots.txt). OLG Hamburg ruled that a reservation written in plain language in terms of use is not machine-readable (5 U 104/24, Dec 2025, [heise](https://heise.de/-11111575)).
- [§ 60d](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__60d.html): TDM for scientific research is also open to "einzelne Forscher, sofern sie nicht kommerzielle Zwecke verfolgen". Rights holders cannot opt out, and contracts cannot restrict it ([§ 60g](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__60g.html)). Individual researchers get no right to keep the copies (§ 60d(5)).
- YouTube: the EEA terms (effective 2022-01-05) forbid downloading except "as specifically permitted by the Service", with prior written permission, or "as permitted by applicable law". They also forbid circumventing features that "prevent or restrict the copying" ([terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms?gl=DE&hl=en); the US version has no exception for applicable law). OLG Hamburg ruled that the "rolling cipher" is an effective technical measure and that youtube-dl users act in bad faith (5 U 54/23, 2024-11-21, [heise](https://heise.de/-10181247)). This applies to CC BY videos too.
- Lowest-risk path: play the Mediathek stream live into the app, so no audio file exists (transient copies, [§ 44a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__44a.html)). If a file is needed, download only from the Mediathek and never from YouTube. Keep audio, transcripts and labels local, delete them after the run, and commit only aggregate numbers.

## Recommendation

1. Proposed mix for the repo set (about 100 segments): about 30 Bundestag segments (Regierungsbefragung and Fragestunde of one session, plus the "Jugend und Parlament" closing discussion). About 15 Landtag segments (BW Fragestunde and Aktuelle Debatte with BW video; Bayern Zwischenbemerkungen with audio kept local). About 30 from panels and debates (RLS Lieferkettengesetz, taz lab 2026, WikiCon 2023 Streitgespräch, Fusion 2026, CCC-Jahresrückblick). About 15 from "Blaue Couch" interviews as realistic negatives, and about 10 from teens (Jugend hackt 2025 and Jugend und Parlament). Text always comes from our STT; the protocol serves as a cross-check where one exists.
2. Add one `SOURCES.md` row per source with its licence and attribution (title, author, source, licence), and mark BY-SA items. Commit audio clips only from CC BY or BY-SA sources (Commons, or media.ccc.de pages with a licence line). Bundestag and BW clips only if the owner accepts their purpose clauses. No clips from Bayern, Berlin or NRW, and none of minors.
3. Never fetch from YouTube. For a CC BY YouTube video, use its Commons copy.
4. Talk shows stay local, as in § 4. Prefer ZDF and phoenix shows; use ARD shows only by live replay. Report numbers only.
5. Check the balance per source: RLS and taz lean left, so add the Bundestag (all parties), CCC and WikiCon; also count men and women per source.
