## SSRF guard (brief 15.5) – refused
- schemes other than http/https (file, gopher, data), credentials in URLs, ports other than 80/443
- internal names: localhost, *.localhost, *.local, *.internal, *.home.arpa, single-label (redis,
  postgres, searxng, gateway), host.docker.internal, metadata.google.internal
- IP literals in every notation: 127.0.0.1, decimal 2130706433, hex 0x7f000001, [::1],
  [::ffff:127.0.0.1]; blocked ranges incl. 169.254.169.254, CGNAT, TEST-NETs, multicast,
  fc00::/7, fe80::/10, IPv4-mapped / NAT64 / 6to4 embedding of internal IPv4
- names resolving to an internal address, or to a mix of public and internal (rebinding):
  validation runs inside the connection's own DNS lookup, so checked = connected address
- redirects to the metadata IP or an internal name (without connecting), > 3 redirects
- bodies above the limit (with and without content-length), non-accepted content types, timeouts
- the production policy blocks the loopback fake server outright

## Other behaviour
- robots.txt (RFC 9309): own group vs * group, wildcards, $ anchors, longest match, Allow wins
  ties; 4xx → allow, 5xx/timeout/blocked → disallow; cached once per origin
- extraction: scripts not executed, nav/footer dropped, publisher/publish date read,
  injected instructions stay plain text (the prompt delimits them)
- chunking: every chunk ≤ 500 tokens, all but the last ≥ 300, one sentence overlap, full coverage;
  ordinals ("8. Mai") vs years ("1945.") handled
- tiers: longest-suffix match from config/source-tiers.yaml; notbund.de is not bund.de
- Google Fact Check: key only in the X-Goog-Api-Key header; entries without http(s) URL or rating dropped
- Wikidata: dates by precision in German (incl. v. Chr.), counts de-DE formatted, only allowlisted properties
- researchClaim: tiers in parallel, a failing or timed-out tier is reported and dropped
- caches: repeated fetch/search served from cache, failures never cached
- ranking: shared-word chunk first, score = similarity × tier weight
