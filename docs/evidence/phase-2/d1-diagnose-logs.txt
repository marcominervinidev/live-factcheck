Erkennung · D1 – diagnose logs for detection drops and research (2026-10-02, branch feat/d1-diagnose-logs)
=========================================================================================================

Plan .ai/plans/feature-erkennung-absatz.md (grilling round 3, decisions 17 and 18), task D1; #79
(drops were counted but never logged), #80 (the "claim checked" line names the reason, not where
the evidence got lost).

Trigger: in the owner's test run of 2026-10-02 eight segments ended as provider_error, and nothing
said whether Jev or Claude failed; later two claims ended no_evidence although a SearXNG query for
the Berlin rents returned 26 results (among them Tagesspiegel: "binnen zehn Jahren um 69 Prozent").

What the logs now say (never segment text, claim text, queries or URLs):
- claim-extractor, one line per dropped segment, msg "segment dropped":
    {"sessionId":…,"segmentId":…,"reason":"not_a_claim"}                                   (info)
    {"sessionId":…,"segmentId":…,"reason":"provider_error",
     "failure":{"source":"classifier","kind":"provider_error","status":401,"error":"AuthenticationError"}}   (warn)
- fact-checker, one line per researched check, msg "research summary" (shape asserted by the unit
  test "shows where a check without evidence lost it"):
    {"sessionId":…,"claimId":…,"queries":3,"fallbackQueries":false,
     "documents":{"wikipedia":0,"web":0},"factChecks":0,
     "failedSources":["wikidata: FetchFailedError 429"],
     "web":{"queries":2,"failedQueries":0,"results":8,"pages":5,"skipped":{"http_403":5}},
     "snippets":0,"retrieveMs":…,"classifyMs":0}
  `relevant` (snippets judged relevant) and `sufficient` (probability that the evidence suffices)
  appear once those steps ran. A cached verdict does no research and logs no summary.
