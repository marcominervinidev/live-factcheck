1. Source and extraction
   Plenarprotokoll 21/95 (23.09.2026), XML https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/1217366/21095.xml,
   Tagesordnungspunkt 1 „Befragung der Bundesregierung“, `rede` elements 2–9 (zero-based; turns 3–10):
   Nolte, Pistorius, Nolte, Pistorius, Erndl, Pistorius, Erndl, Pistorius, with the president's lines and
   the worded interjections in between. Element classes decided one by one (SOURCES.md).
   Extraction: a session script (stdlib xml.etree, not in the repo): spoken paragraphs split at sentence
   ends followed by an ordinary space and a capital, ordinal numbers and abbreviations re-joined; only
   ordinary whitespace collapsed, so no-break spaces stay as published (13 rows carry them).
